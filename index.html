Projects with a Purpose

★ Featured · AI for Education Access · Aug 2026

Gaza & Palestine Scholarship Finder — AI Agent

The Problem

Talented Palestinian students lose life-changing scholarships — not for lack of merit, but because funding information is scattered across hundreds of sites, deadlines pass unseen, and generic search can't match eligibility rules for Gaza & the West Bank.

My Solution

A multi-agent AI platform: an Orchestrator Agent coordinating a Text-to-SQL expert, a Vector Search expert (semantic search over 1536-dim embeddings), and a DB Write expert — behind a split-screen UI with a real-time AI assistant (Flask-SocketIO + OpenRouter).

The Impact

49 fully funded scholarships indexed and instantly searchable. Students ask in plain language — like "emergency grants for Gaza students" — and get matched in seconds. Designed to scale to thousands of students.

  • My role: designed and developed the platform end-to-end — multi-agent architecture, prompt engineering, backend, and UI.
  • What I learned: grounding LLMs in structured data to prevent hallucination, orchestrating specialized agents, and designing for users in crisis.
  • Why it matters for my research: this project is the seed of my Master's question — how multi-agent LLM systems can reliably serve low-resource, high-need communities.

03.

Responsible AI

Building AI for a vulnerable population is a responsibility, not just a technical challenge. The Scholarship Finder is designed to earn the trust of students who cannot afford bad information:

🎯 Grounded, Not Guessing

The assistant answers from a verified scholarship database (Text-to-SQL + semantic search) rather than free-generating text — minimizing hallucination when the stakes are a student's future.

🔒 Privacy by Design

No sensitive personal data is collected. Only scholarship bookmarks are stored — the minimum needed to be useful.

✅ Human-Verified Data

Every scholarship entry is manually checked — funding, eligibility, and deadlines — before it enters the database.
